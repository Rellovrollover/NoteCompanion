2nd Brain — privacy

A personal notes app used by its author only.

What it stores

Notes are kept in the browser on your own device and synced to a folder in your own Microsoft OneDrive. Nothing is sent to any server run by this app; there isn't one.

Google Calendar

If you sign in with Google, the app reads events from your calendars to show today's schedule and to suggest teaching sessions for your portfolio, and creates events only when you ask it to. Calendar data is cached on your device only. The Google sign-in token stays on the device and can be revoked at any time from your Google account or from the app's Settings.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. No analytics or tracking.
